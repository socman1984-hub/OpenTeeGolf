Play any course in the world, free.

OpenTeeGolf is an open-source golf simulator for the FlightScope Mevo+. It builds playable 3D courses from OpenStreetMap, with real terrain, aerial imagery, woods, rough, streams and live weather, and it will always be free.

What it does

Everything runs on your own PC. The game opens in your browser and courses are built from public map data.

🗺️Any mapped course

Search OpenStreetMap for a course and build it in a few minutes: greens, fairways, bunkers, tees, ponds, streams, cart paths, roads, car parks and buildings, with real elevation. A checklist shows each step as it builds.

🌳Real-world detail

Trees placed from canopy-height data and the aerial photo, with undergrowth along the woods. Rough you can see blade by blade up close, tall native grass, streams that flow downhill, and wooden bridges where cart paths cross water.

📡Mevo+ support

Connect straight to the Mevo+ over its Wi-Fi, with no other software (experimental), or through FS Golf. There's a live camera and level screen for setting up.

⛳Ball flight and bounce

Physics for carry, spin, wind and altitude, with the real weather at the course by default. Bounce, roll and backspin that change with the surface, trees and flagsticks to hit, a slope grid for reading putts, and a driving range, target greens and putt-putt course for practice.

✏️Course editor

After each build, a quick review of any trees standing in the line of play. Move tees, pins and aim points, reshape greens and bunkers, and plant or remove trees. Update a course whenever its map improves.

🔄Free and updating

Open source under the GPL-3.0, so it stays free. The game tells you when a new version is out and installs it in one click.

Recently added

OpenTeeGolf is in alpha and changes every week. The release notes have the full list.

  • Play with friends. Save player profiles (name, handicap, tees) and pick who's playing: stroke play, Stableford, match play, skins, best ball, scramble or alternate shot, with handicaps if you like.
  • Boom range, a game for kids. Blow things up! TNT barrels, crates, watermelons, pumpkins, balloons, a castle and a giant bomb stand down the range, and barrels set off everything near them. Easy, Medium and Hard: on Easy the targets are giant and near misses count.
  • More launch monitors (experimental): the Garmin Approach R10 and Square Golf over Bluetooth, through open-source bridges, plus any device that speaks the open Flight Relay Protocol.
  • Cartoon graphics for slower computers: bright painted turf with bold fairway stripes, round toy trees and no grass blades, much less to draw.
  • Download sizes while loading: each step shows how much it has downloaded and how fast, and when a map or imagery server is slow to answer.
  • Fish and turtles in the ponds. Each pond has the fish of its region (bass, bluegill, carp and more; Europe has its own) and some have turtles paddling about or sunning on a log. Every pond is its own depth and clarity, and a splash throws up a crown of water.
  • Creeks with pools, riffles and boulders. Streams run shallow and fast over riffles and slow in deep pools, with boulders the leaves and foam slide round. Trees no longer stand in the water.
  • Ponds you can see into. Clear water over a stony bed, deeper and darker towards the middle, with lily pads and water lilies near the shore and koi gliding about below. Rings spread across the water where your ball splashes in.
  • Clear creeks you can see into. Streams now show their stony bed through the water, cloudier after rain, with sunlight dancing on the bottom and leaves and flecks of foam drifting downstream.
  • Creek sounds. The game’s first sound: a trickle, a running creek or rapids, depending on how fast the water near you runs. Volume is under Options → Display.
  • Time of day and weather you can change as you play. Early morning to sunset, with the sun placed for the course’s real latitude and today’s date, plus overcast, fog and storms. Press L mid-round to change either.
  • Real clouds. Fair-weather to overcast skies that drift with the wind; when a cloud crosses the sun the light fades and the shadows soften, and on High their shadows sweep across the course and sun rays stream through the trees.
  • Photo-scanned leaves and bark for live oaks, magnolias, sycamores and cypresses, and softer, more natural light under the sky.
  • Faster loading: getting the graphics ready takes about a third less time.
  • Practice areas: a driving range with mountains behind it, target greens at different distances that remember where you were aiming, and a putt-putt course with obstacles. No course needed.
  • A new main menu, with courses, round setup and practice each in their own place.
  • Desert courses look like the desert, with sand and rock between the holes, and creeks that run downhill. In the editor, fairways, greens and rough can each take their colours from the aerial photo.
  • Hard putts lip out, and the game runs faster on courses with lots of tall grass.

Get started

No installer and no account.

  1. Download the latest versionA zip of about 45 MB from the releases page. It includes everything it needs.
  2. Unzip it anywhereFor example to your Documents or Desktop.
  3. Double-click Start OpenTeeGolf.batThe game opens in your browser at localhost:8080. Keep the small black window open while you play. The first time, Windows may say “Windows protected your PC”: choose More info → Run anyway. If the firewall asks about Node.js, allow it on private networks.
  4. Build a courseChoose “Build a course from OpenStreetMap”, search for a course by name, and build it.
  5. Connect your Mevo+Join the PC to the Mevo+ Wi-Fi and choose “Mevo+ direct” in the menu, or use FS Golf. You can also play with the built-in test shots.

What you need

  • Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit
  • Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge (for WebGPU graphics)
  • A graphics card from the last few years
  • An internet connection to build courses (playing a built course works offline)
  • A FlightScope Mevo+ to hit real shots (optional)
