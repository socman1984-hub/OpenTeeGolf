Play any course in the world, free.

OpenTeeGolf is an open-source golf simulator built in Unreal Engine 5. It turns any course mapped in OpenStreetMap into a playable 3D course, with real terrain, woods, water and live weather, for the FlightScope Mevo+ and other launch monitors. It will always be free.

What it does

A game you install and play on your own PC. Courses are built from public map data, in the background while you play.

🌍Every course on one map

All 39,682 golf courses mapped in OpenStreetMap, on a world map. Search, check what's mapped and build it: greens, fairways, bunkers, tees, water, cart paths, roads, car parks and houses, on the real terrain. Queue as many as you like; they build while you play.

📡Your launch monitor

The FlightScope Mevo+ straight over its Wi-Fi or through FS Golf, the Garmin Approach R10 and Square Golf through the bridges that come with the game, the Rapsodo MLM2PRO through the free MLM2PRO Connector, and many more through rēlā. Every shot's numbers as tiles on screen, which ones and where are up to you.

⛳Ball flight and bounce

The open-source OpenFairway physics for carry, spin, wind and altitude. Each surface plays its own way: the ball checks up in the rough, plugs in sand, and bounces and runs on cart paths.

⛈️Weather and time of day

Live weather at the course or any preset, from calm to storm. Clouds drift with the wind and shade the course, rain soaks the grass and greys out the far end of the hole, storms bring lightning and thunder, and "Changing" weather brings showers and sun during the round. The sun sits where it really is at any time of day.

🏌️Practice

A driving range with target greens from 50 to 250 yards, every shot's numbers and your averages per club, and nine holes of putt-putt with a windmill, tunnel, ramps and a pond.

👥Play with friends

Players with their own colour, hand and tees, and the round set up in one place: tees, pins, weather, players and game. Search your courses by name or place and sort them by name, last played or newest. Online games too, hosted by one of you and joined by address, taking turns or all at once.

✏️Course editor

Holes, tees, pins and par; greens, fairways, bunkers and water; trees, tall grass and buildings, drawn on the course's aerial photo. Click a patch of tall grass to give it a grass biome, or a group of trees to make a forest, and join forests together. Most changes reach the game in seconds, and you see what you've changed before you save.

🎨Course designer

Press R in a round and change the course as you watch: the colours of the fairways, greens and rough, what the rough and tall grass are made of, and any tree or rock, one at a time or all of a kind.

🔄Free and updating

Open source under the GPL-3.0, so it stays free. The game tells you when a new version is out and updates itself, downloading only what changed.

Recently added

OpenTeeGolf is in alpha and changes every week. The release notes have the full list.

  • 0.31: real ground under the rough. Close up, the ground between the rough's blades is moss, soil and twigs instead of a bright, wavy pattern, and the deep rough is a greener grey instead of a pale straw patch. Low sun no longer leaves blocky shadows across the tees, the houses' windows no longer seem to blink from afar, and every choice in the course editor is a dropdown, with the game's guess for each building spelled out. The frame-rate bar (P) now shows where the camera is.
  • 0.30: a real scorecard, truer roll and deeper rough. After each hole the scorecard looks like a club's own card, with each hole's yardage from your tees, birdies circled and bogeys boxed, and a countdown to the next hole. Tick a box (or turn it on in Settings) to go straight to the next hole without the card. The ball no longer creeps down sloping fairways for ages: it settles into the grass and stops, and each cut, from the collar to the deep rough, holds it a little more. The minimap zooms in as you near the green, with + and − buttons to zoom further. The auto-putt circle is bolder and turns green when your ball stops inside it. In the designer, a picked tree shows which forest it belongs to, with a button to pick that forest. The rough has more depth: its blades shade each other and the ground, darker down among the roots. Grass never grows in the bunkers, tees and fairways with the game's colours no longer show what was in the aerial photo (parked carts, straw-coloured rough), mowing stripes run one way across each tee, fairway and green, and every choice in the editors is now a dropdown list.
  • 0.29.1: the rough fixed. From high up or far off, the rough showed as a dark navy blue, with a bright line where the course met the land round it. It's green again. There's a Linux test build of 0.29.1 too.
  • 0.29: online games, Rapsodo MLM2PRO and bridges. Play a round with friends on other computers: one of you hosts from the Online page and the others join with the host's address, on the same network or over the internet with a port forwarded on the host's router. Anyone missing the course gets it from the host, only the files they don't have. Take turns and watch each other's shots, or all play at once with everyone's ball and tracer on screen. Play with the Rapsodo MLM2PRO through the free MLM2PRO Connector, build bridges over creeks in a round, and in the course editor click a patch of tall grass to give it its own grass biome, join forests together and see exactly what you've changed before you save. Rain now falls wherever you look, and storms bring a grey mist. There's also a Linux test build of 0.29.
  • 0.28: auto-putt and updates on start. Turn on auto-putt and the hole ends once you're on the green: 1 putt inside the circle round the hole, 2 outside. The game checks for a new version as it starts and offers to update there and then. The flight camera follows behind the ball again and turns toward the flag as the ball comes down, and trees in the way while you aim are now see-through.
  • 0.27: new ball physics and a TV camera. The latest OpenFairway physics, fitted to real shot data, so carries, landings and roll-out are closer to the real thing. A camera that keeps the green in view as the ball comes down, trees in the way hidden while you aim, pictures of every grass in the biome studio, and forests and grass biomes you can change during a round.
  • 0.26.2: rēlā. Play with any launch monitor rēlā supports, such as SkyTrak, GC2, FlightScope, Nova, Mevo+, R10 or Square: pick rēlā in the game's Launch monitor panel, and in rēlā send to this PC, port 921.
  • 0.26.1: the Course builder. New course is now the Course builder, and courses already in the game are edited from the Play page. The grass biome studio has tidy checkboxes, each kind folded until you open it, and a free camera you fly with W A S D. The course editor's biome and forest choices are dropdowns.
  • Unreal edition 0.26: Mevo+ live view and fast shots. See the Mevo+ camera in the game's Launch monitor panel to line up your ball, and turn on fast shots so full swings take off sooner. The shot data now shows both of the Mevo+'s spin readings, and the Mevo+ reads spin on every full swing.
  • Unreal edition 0.25: grass biomes and forests. Mix tall grasses, wildflowers, heather and ferns into your own grass biomes, and tree species into forests, with a live 3D preview as you change them. Then in the course editor give a course's tall grass a biome, draw areas with their own, and click a group of trees to make it a forest you can thicken, thin or replant in one go.

Get started

No account, and no admin rights needed.

  1. Download the installerUse the download button at the top, or get OpenTeeGolf-Setup from the releases page (about 1 GB).
  2. Run itThe first time, Windows may say “Windows protected your PC”: choose More info → Run anyway. The game updates itself from then on.
  3. Build a courseOpen New course, find a course on the world map or search for it by name, and build it. You can keep playing while it builds.
  4. Connect your launch monitorJoin the PC to the Mevo+ Wi-Fi, or use FS Golf; the Garmin R10 and Square Golf connect through the bridges that come with the game, and the Rapsodo MLM2PRO through the MLM2PRO Connector. You can also play with the built-in test shots (Space or T).
  5. Tee offPick your course, players, tees and weather, and press Start.

What you need

  • Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit (or Linux on x86-64 with Vulkan, using the test build)
  • A graphics card from the last few years (DirectX 12)
  • A few GB of free disk space
  • An internet connection to build courses (playing a built course works offline)
  • A launch monitor to hit real shots (optional): FlightScope Mevo+, Garmin Approach R10, Square Golf or Rapsodo MLM2PRO, or any launch monitor rēlā supports

For developers: pieces you can reuse

The game's self-contained parts are Unreal Engine plugins, each with a README on how it works and how to use it in your own project. They don't depend on the game, so you can copy one folder into your project's Plugins and use it. The rest of the game's code is organised by area, with a README for each: start here.

⛳Ball physics

The ball's flight, bounce and roll (the OpenFairway model), and a course for it to play on. Plain C++.

OTGBallPhysics

📡Launch monitor client

Shots from FS Golf and other OpenConnect apps (rēlā and the MLM2PRO Connector among them), straight from the Mevo+ over its Wi-Fi, and from Flight Relay Protocol devices like the Garmin R10.

OTGLaunchMonitor

🗺️Minimap

A hole's map from its aerial photo, turned so the hole runs up the screen, with the yardages and click or drag to aim.

OTGMinimap

🧭Wind box

An arrow showing how the wind blows against your aim, with its speed and how it plays.

OTGWindIndicator

☄️Shot tracer

The ball's flight drawn as a ribbon that faces the camera and stays readable from the tee.

OTGShotTracer

⛈️Weather sound

Rain, wind and rolling thunder made as they play, with no recordings to license.

OTGWeatherSound

📦.glb reader

Reads glTF binary models at run time: positions, normals, colours, UVs and triangles.

OTGGlb

🎨UI kit

The game's Slate look: fonts, colours, glass panels and rounded buttons.

OTGUiKit
