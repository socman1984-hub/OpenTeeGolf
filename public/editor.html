Tree species
    Add trees: click the map once per tree. Tree area: click its outline, double-click or Enter to finish; it fills with trees (not on fairways, greens, tees, bunkers or water). Your trees are the bright ones: drag to move, Delete to remove.
    Help
    • Drag the map to pan, scroll to zoom.
    • Drag tees, pins, aim points and the green center of the selected hole.
    • Click a shape to select it. Drag its corners; drag a small circle to add a corner; Alt+click a corner to remove it.
    • Drag inside a selected shape to move the whole shape (to line it up with the photo); arrow keys nudge it 25 cm, Shift+arrow 1 m.
    • Trees: dark circles are found from tree heights and OpenStreetMap, amber dashed ones are guessed from the aerial photo, and bright ones are yours. Drag yours to move them. Click a found tree to remove it or replace it with your own; a red cross marks a removed tree (click it to put the tree back).
    • Delete removes the selected shape or tree · Ctrl+Z undo · Ctrl+S save · Esc cancel.
